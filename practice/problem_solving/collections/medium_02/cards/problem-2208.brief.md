# Minimum Operations to Halve Array Sum

Given an array of positive integers, one operation chooses a current array
value and replaces it with **exactly half** of that value. The half may be
fractional and may be chosen again in a later operation. Return the fewest
operations needed to reduce the array's total sum by at least half of its
**original** total.

The array has between 1 and `10^5` elements, each initially between 1 and
`10^7`. An exact half of the original total meets the goal.
