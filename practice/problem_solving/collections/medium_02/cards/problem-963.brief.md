# Minimum Area Rectangle II

Given distinct points in the plane, choose four of them that form the corners
of a rectangle. Its sides may have **any orientation**; they need not be
parallel to the coordinate axes. Return the smallest rectangle area, or `0`
if no rectangle can be formed. An answer within `10^-5` of the true area is
accepted.

There are between 1 and 50 points. Each coordinate is an integer from `0`
through `4 × 10^4`. For example, the points `(1,2)`, `(2,1)`, `(1,0)`, and
`(0,1)` form a rotated rectangle of area `2`.
