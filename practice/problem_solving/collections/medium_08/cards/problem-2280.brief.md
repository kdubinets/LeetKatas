# Minimum Lines to Represent a Line Chart

Each pair in `stockPrices` gives a distinct day and its price. Plot the points in increasing day order and connect consecutive points with straight segments. Return the fewest straight **segments in sequence** needed to represent that same polyline: adjacent collinear segments may be combined. Disconnected portions are not combined merely because they lie on the same infinite line. A single point needs zero segments.

There are 1 to 100000 points; day and price values lie from 1 to `10^9`. Input order need not be chronological.
