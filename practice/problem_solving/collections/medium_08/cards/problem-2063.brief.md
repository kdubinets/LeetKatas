# Vowels of All Substrings

Return the sum of vowel counts over **all nonempty substring occurrences** of lowercase string `word`. Vowels are `a,e,i,o,u`. Equal substring text at different positions counts separately.

For `word="ab"`, the substrings `"a"`, `"ab"`, and `"b"` contribute 1, 1, and 0, giving 2.

`1 <= |word| <= 100000`; the result may require a wide integer.
