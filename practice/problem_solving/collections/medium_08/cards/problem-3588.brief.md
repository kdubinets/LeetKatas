# Find Maximum Area of a Triangle

Given distinct points `coords`, choose three that form a positive-area triangle with at least one side parallel to the x-axis or y-axis. Return **twice** the greatest possible area, or -1 if no such triangle exists. The third vertex can be on either side of the parallel side.

`1 <= n <= 100000`; each coordinate is between 1 and `10^6`. Zero-area triangles are invalid; the doubled area may require a wide integer.
