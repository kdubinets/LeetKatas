# Minimum Cost to Make Arrays Identical

Two integer arrays `arr` and `brr` have equal length. You may pay a fixed cost `k` to split `arr` into any number of contiguous pieces and reorder those pieces arbitrarily; this may be done any number of times. You may also change any one element by an integer amount, paying the absolute amount changed. Return the minimum cost to make `arr` equal `brr` position by position. Array length is at most 100,000; values lie in `[-10^5,10^5]`, and `k` may be as large as 2×10^10.

Splitting into single-element pieces is allowed.
