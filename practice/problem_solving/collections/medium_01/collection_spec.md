# Medium Level C 01

This language-neutral Level C collection contains 11 earlier medium cards from
the reviewed foundation collection, followed by 14 cards chosen through the
reproducible random candidate draw in `../medium_random_selection.json`. The
earlier cards retain their prior relative order. The collection is full.

Only medium problems belong here. The collection holds at most 25 cards.
Further reviewed cards belong in `medium_02`, which has its own stable
collection ID. Do not move a published card to rebalance cohorts, since the
collection ID is part of its practice identity.

Each public `.brief.md` contains only the focused problem statement. Its paired
`.card.json` holds the hidden hint, outline, metadata, and source provenance.
Validate with `src/scripts/validate_level_c_collection.py` before publication.
