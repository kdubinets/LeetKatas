# Delete and Earn

Given an array `nums` of positive integers, repeatedly choose an occurrence of a value `x`, remove it, and earn `x` points. That choice also removes every remaining occurrence of `x-1` and `x+1`, earning nothing for those removals. You may stop at any time. Return the maximum total points.

`1 <= |nums| <= 2 * 10^4`; `1 <= nums[i] <= 10^4`.
