# Circle and Rectangle Overlapping

Given a circular disk with radius `radius` and center `(xCenter,yCenter)`, and an axis-aligned rectangle with bottom-left `(x1,y1)` and top-right `(x2,y2)`, determine whether they share any point. Both shapes include their boundaries, so touching counts.

`1 <= radius <= 2000`; every coordinate lies in `[-10^4,10^4]`; `x1 < x2` and `y1 < y2`.
