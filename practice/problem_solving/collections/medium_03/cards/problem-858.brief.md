# Mirror Reflection

A laser starts at the southwest corner of a square mirrored room of side length `p`. Its first contact with the east wall is `q` units above the southeast corner. It reflects from the walls; return the numbered corner receptor it first reaches. The receptors are **0 at southeast**, **1 at northeast**, and **2 at northwest**; southwest is the source and has no receptor.

`1 ≤ q ≤ p ≤ 1,000`. The ray is guaranteed to reach a receptor. For example, `p=2, q=1` reaches receptor `2`.
