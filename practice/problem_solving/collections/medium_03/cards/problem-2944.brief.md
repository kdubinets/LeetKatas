# Minimum Number of Coins for Fruits

Fruits are numbered `1` through `n`; buying fruit `j` costs `prices[j−1]` and lets you take **any number of the next `j` fruits** for free. You need all fruits at minimum total cost. A fruit eligible for free may still be purchased to earn its own free-fruit benefit.

`1 ≤ n ≤ 1,000`, prices are positive and at most `100,000`. For `[3,1,2]`, the minimum is `4`: buy fruits 1 and 2, then take fruit 3 free.
