# Airplane Seat Assignment Probability

An airplane has `n` passengers and `n` numbered seats, one assigned to each passenger. Passenger 1 has lost their ticket and picks a free seat uniformly at random. Each later passenger takes their assigned seat if free; otherwise they choose uniformly from the remaining free seats. Return the probability that passenger `n` receives their assigned seat.

`1 ≤ n ≤ 100,000`. At `n=1`, the probability is 1.
