# Count Cells in Overlapping Horizontal and Vertical Substrings

An `m × n` grid of lowercase English letters and a lowercase English-letter `pattern` are given. A horizontal substring reads left to right and, after a row ends, continues at the first cell of the next row. A vertical substring reads top to bottom and, after a column ends, continues at the top of the next column. Neither reading wraps from its final cell back to its first. Count cells belonging to at least one occurrence of `pattern` in **each** reading order. Occurrences may overlap.

`1 <= m,n <= 1000`; `m*n <= 100000`; `1 <= |pattern| <= m*n`.
