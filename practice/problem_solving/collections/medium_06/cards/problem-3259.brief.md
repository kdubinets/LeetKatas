# Maximum Energy Boost From Two Drinks

For each of `n` hours, drinks A and B offer positive energy amounts `energyDrinkA[i]` and `energyDrinkB[i]`. You may start with either drink. During an hour you may drink one type, but changing types requires an intervening hour of cleansing with **no** energy. Maximize total energy over the `n` hours.

`3 <= n <= 100000`; each energy value is from `1` to `100000`. The total may exceed 32-bit range.
