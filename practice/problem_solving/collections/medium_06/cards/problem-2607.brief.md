# Make K-Subarray Sums Equal

An integer array `arr` is circular, so a length-`k` subarray may cross the end and continue at the start. In one operation you may increase or decrease any one element by one. Find the minimum operations needed to make the sums of **all** circular length-`k` subarrays equal.

`1 <= k <= |arr| <= 100000`; `1 <= arr[i] <= 1000000000`. The answer may require a wide integer.
