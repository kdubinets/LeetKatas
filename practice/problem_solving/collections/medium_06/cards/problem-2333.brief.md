# Minimum Sum of Squared Difference

Two arrays `nums1` and `nums2` of length `n` are given. You may increase or decrease entries of the first array at most `k1` times and entries of the second at most `k2` times, by one per operation. Values may become negative. Minimize the sum of squared differences across matching indices; unused operations are allowed.

`1 <= n <= 100000`; original entries lie in `[0,100000]`; `0 <= k1,k2 <= 1000000000`. Return a wide integer.
