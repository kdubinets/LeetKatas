# Regions Cut By Slashes

An `n × n` grid of unit squares contains `/`, `\`, or a blank space in each square. A slash is a wall running between the corresponding opposite corners of that square; blank squares have no interior wall. Count the connected regions of the grid interior separated by these walls. Adjacent squares can connect across their shared edge, but regions cannot cross a drawn slash.

For example, one square containing `/` or `\` has two regions, while one blank square has one. `1 <= n <= 30`.
