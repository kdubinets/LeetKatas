# Minimum Absolute Sum Difference

Given equally long positive integer arrays `nums1` and `nums2`, minimize the sum of `|nums1[i]-nums2[i]|`. You may replace at most one entry of `nums1` with a value occurring anywhere in the original `nums1`; the supplying occurrence is not removed. Return the minimum sum modulo `10^9+7`, choosing the minimum before reducing it.

`1 <= n <= 100000` and `1 <= nums1[i], nums2[i] <= 100000`.
