# Minimum Cost to Acquire Required Items

Three unlimited item types are available. One costs `cost1` and supplies one unit of requirement 1 only; one costs `cost2` and supplies one unit of requirement 2 only; one costs `costBoth` and supplies one unit of each requirement. Meet at least `need1` and `need2` units at minimum total cost, and return that cost. Extra units are permitted.

`1 <= cost1, cost2, costBoth <= 10^6` and `0 <= need1, need2 <= 10^9`.
