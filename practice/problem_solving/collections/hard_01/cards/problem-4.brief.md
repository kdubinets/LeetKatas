# Median of Two Sorted Arrays

Given two arrays sorted in nondecreasing order, return the median of all their
values as though they formed one combined sorted sequence. If the combined
length is even, the median is the average of its two middle values.

The arrays have lengths `m` and `n`, either array may be empty, and
`1 <= m + n <= 2000`. Values lie between `-10^6` and `10^6`. The required
overall running time is `O(log(m + n))`.
